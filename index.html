Hello, I'm

Tzu-Hao Huang 黃子豪

Ph.D. Student · Wireless Communications & Deep Learning

National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan

I am a Ph.D. student working at the intersection of 5G/6G wireless communications and deep learning. My research spans integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), deep-learning-based CSI feedback for MIMO systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS), and mmWave/THz communications. I enjoy turning hard signal-processing problems into elegant, data-driven solutions.

Research Interests

5G / 6G Communications

Physical-layer design and signal processing for next-generation wireless networks.

Integrated Sensing & Communication

ISAC systems that jointly perform sensing, localization, and data transmission.

CSI Feedback

Deep-learning-based compression and reconstruction of channel state information for MIMO.

Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces

Signal optimization and beamforming with RIS to reshape the wireless propagation environment.

mmWave & THz

Beamforming and channel modeling for millimeter-wave and terahertz frequency bands.

Digital Twin & AI

Leveraging digital twins and deep learning for AI-native wireless system design.
