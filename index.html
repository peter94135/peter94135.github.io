About Me

Hi, I'm Tzu-Hao Huang (黃子豪).

I received the M.Eng. degree from the Institute of Communications Engineering, National Sun Yat-sen University (NSYSU), Kaohsiung, Taiwan, in 2023, ranking 1st of 31 in my class. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree at the Institute of Electrical and Control Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Hsinchu, Taiwan.

My research interests include AI-integrated communication systems, digital twins, and signal processing — deep-learning CSI feedback, radio-map and cross-band signal prediction, collaborative MIMO, UAV localization, and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces.

Research Interests

AI-Integrated Communication Systems

Deep learning inside the 5G/6G physical layer — learning-based feedback, prediction and optimization for next-generation networks.

Deep-Learning CSI Feedback

Compressing MIMO channel state information and precoding matrices (e.g. EVCsiNet) to cut feedback overhead while raising throughput.

Digital Twins & Ray Tracing

Site-specific virtual channels built from 3D models and accurate antennas, validated against real-world measurements with online learning.

Radio Maps & Signal Prediction

Predicting MIMO throughput from geographic data and cross-band, multi-directional signal strength from FR1 to FR3 with sparse measurements.

Collaborative MIMO & RIS

User-centric collaborative antenna arrays across personal devices, and phase optimization for reconfigurable intelligent surfaces.

UAV Localization

GNSS-free UAV positioning in urban canyons by fusing cellular RSS fingerprints with IMU trajectories over sliding windows.
