Hello, I'm

Tzu-Hao Huang

M.S. Student · Wireless Communications & Deep Learning

I am a graduate researcher working at the intersection of 5G/6G wireless communications and deep learning. My current research focuses on massive MIMO, channel charting, and CSI feedback compression. I enjoy turning hard signal-processing problems into elegant, data-driven solutions.

Research Interests

Massive MIMO

Beamforming, precoding, and signal processing for large-scale antenna systems in 5G/6G networks.

Channel Charting

Unsupervised learning of low-dimensional channel representations for localization and mobility.

CSI Feedback

Deep-learning-based compression and reconstruction of channel state information.

Deep Learning

Neural architectures, representation learning, and their application to physical-layer problems.
